# Animation Reflection

Animations were used where they served a clear purpose.
The hero fade-in draws attention to the headline immediately on load,
giving the page a polished, professional first impression.
The floating shape adds depth to an otherwise flat background without
distracting from the main message. The scroll-triggered reveal on
feature cards makes the page feel interactive and guides the user's
eye naturally downward. Care was taken to keep animations subtle and
short — anything longer than 0.6s or purely decorative was avoided,
since excessive motion shifts focus away from the content itself.
